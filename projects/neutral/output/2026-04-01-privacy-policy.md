## 1. Who We Are

AI Office, Inc. ("AI Office", "we", "us", or "our") operates the AI Office platform — a B2B SaaS product that provides AI-powered agent tools to business customers. Our registered address and Data Protection Officer contact are listed in Section 13.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you use our services, visit our website, or interact with us as a customer, prospective customer, or end user.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **Website visitors** — anyone browsing aioffice.com or related domains
- **Business customers** ("Customers") — companies that subscribe to AI Office
- **End users** — employees, contractors, or agents of our Customers who use the platform
- **Prospective customers** — individuals who request demos, sign up for waitlists, or contact sales

AI Office acts as:
- A **data controller** for data we collect about website visitors and Customer account contacts
- A **data processor** on behalf of Customers for any personal data processed by AI agents or workflows they configure and run within the platform

## 3. Data We Collect

### 3.1 Account and Billing Data
- Name, work email address, company name, job title
- Billing information (processed via a PCI-compliant third-party processor — we do not store raw card numbers)
- Subscription tier, usage history, payment status

### 3.2 Platform Usage Data
- Log data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, timestamps, pages visited, API calls made
- Product usage metrics: features used, session duration, agent runs triggered, error rates
- Support communications: tickets, chat transcripts, emails

### 3.3 Agent-Processed Data (Customer Content)
When Customers deploy AI agents within the platform, those agents may process data provided by or on behalf of the Customer. This may include:
- Documents, emails, calendar entries, or meeting transcripts uploaded or connected by the Customer
- Outputs generated by AI agents (summaries, drafts, structured data)
- Integration data from connected third-party tools (e.g., calendar, CRM, communication platforms)

**AI Office does not use Customer Content to train AI models without explicit written consent.**

### 3.4 Cookies and Tracking
- Strictly necessary cookies (session management, authentication)
- Analytics cookies (usage patterns — opt-out available)
- No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases:

| Processing activity | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and managing your account | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Billing and payment processing | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Sending product updates and security notices | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Sending marketing emails | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — opt-in required |
| Security monitoring and abuse prevention | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Processing Customer Content via agents | Performance of a contract / instructions of controller (Art. 6(1)(b) + Art. 28) |
| Analytics | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), with opt-out available |
| Legal compliance (tax records, breach notification) | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have conducted a balancing test and concluded our interests do not override data subjects' rights. Detailed assessments are available on request.

## 5. How We Use Your Data

We use data to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the AI Office platform
- Authenticate users and manage access control
- Process billing and send invoices
- Respond to support requests
- Send transactional notifications (security alerts, subscription changes, downtime notices)
- Send marketing communications (with consent, opt-out available at any time)
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations (tax, regulatory reporting, litigation holds)
- Conduct aggregate, anonymised product analytics

We do not:
- Sell personal data to third parties
- Use Customer Content for AI model training without explicit written consent
- Use personal data for automated individual decision-making with significant legal effects without human review

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Active account data | Duration of subscription + 30 days |
| Billing records | 7 years (legal/tax obligation) |
| Support tickets | 3 years from closure |
| Security and access logs | 12 months |
| Agent-processed Customer Content | Configurable by Customer (default: deleted within 30 days of subscription end) |

Customers can request deletion of their account data at any time. Deletion of active account data completes within 30 days. Audit logs and billing records are retained for the legally required period regardless of deletion requests.

## 9. Customer Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

When AI Office processes personal data on behalf of a Customer as a data processor, the parties' obligations are governed by a Data Processing Agreement. Our standard DPA:

- Incorporates EU SCCs (Module 2: controller to processor) and UK IDTA
- Defines permitted purposes for sub-processing
- Specifies security measures and audit rights
- Covers breach notification obligations (72 hours to Customer; Customer notifies supervisory authority)
- Includes deletion and return obligations on contract termination

The standard DPA is available at aioffice.com/legal/dpa. Enterprise customers with specific requirements should contact legal@aioffice.com.

**Customers are responsible for:**
- Having a valid legal basis for the personal data they provide to or process through AI Office
- Ensuring end users are informed that their data may be processed by AI Office
- Configuring data retention settings appropriate to their obligations
- Notifying AI Office of any data subject rights requests that require AI Office's assistance

## 10. Your Rights

### 10.1 GDPR Rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

- **Access** — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Restriction** — request that we pause processing while a dispute is resolved
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw at any time without affecting prior lawful processing

To exercise these rights, email privacy@aioffice.com. We will respond within 30 days (extendable to 3 months for complex requests, with notice).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In the EU, the lead supervisory authority is determined by our place of establishment. In the UK, it is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

### 10.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected about you
- Know whether your personal information is sold or disclosed, and to whom
- Say no to the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To submit a verifiable consumer request, email privacy@aioffice.com or use the in-app data controls.

## 11. Security

AI Office implements technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- Role-based access control and least-privilege principles
- Multi-factor authentication enforcement for administrative access
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scanning
- Employee security training and background checks for personnel with data access
- Incident response plan with defined escalation and notification procedures
- SOC 2 Type II audit (target: in progress — see current security posture at aioffice.com/security)

**Breach notification:** In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, we will notify affected Customers within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. We will provide details of the nature of the breach, likely consequences, and measures taken. Customers are responsible for notifying their own supervisory authorities and data subjects as required.
