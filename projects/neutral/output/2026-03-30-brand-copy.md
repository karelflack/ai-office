## Brand Foundation

### Product Name

**Neutral**

The name was already established for this project and it earns its place. "Neutral" communicates calm over chaos, balance over noise, and independence from the hype cycle that defines the AI productivity space. It works as a wordmark. It reads cleanly at small sizes. It has no geographic or cultural baggage. It does not try too hard.

One strategic note: the name doubles as a positioning statement. In a market full of "superpowers," "unfair advantages," and "AI teammates," a brand called Neutral is the one that doesn't need to sell you anything. That confidence is the brand.

---

### Tagline

**Think clearly. Work calmly.**

This is the headline-level expression of the brand. Two short sentences. Each one earned. "Think clearly" addresses the core problem — cognitive overload, context switching, too many tabs. "Work calmly" positions against hustle culture and the anxiety-inducing urgency of most productivity software.

**Alternate taglines (for A/B testing or secondary use):**
- *Less noise. More work.*
- *The AI tool that gets out of your way.*
- *Built for focus. Not for features.*

---

### Brand Voice

**Confident. Direct. Warm without being casual.**

Neutral speaks like a thoughtful senior colleague — not a startup founder hyping their product, and not a corporate HR slide deck. The voice is measured. It makes claims it can back up. It doesn't reach for adjectives when a noun will do.

**Voice principles:**

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Confident | State what Neutral does. Don't hedge. Don't apologize. |
| Direct | Short sentences. No preamble. Lead with the benefit. |
| Warm | Human > clinical. Acknowledge the user's actual experience. |
| Honest | No "save 10 hours a week" unless we can prove it. |
| Quiet | Let the product speak. Copy doesn't need to oversell. |

**Tone examples:**

| Don't say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Leverage AI-powered synergies to unlock productivity" | "Notes that connect themselves." |
| "Join thousands of users who trust Neutral" | "Used daily by researchers, writers, and people who can't afford to lose a thought." |
| "Revolutionary AI meeting assistant" | "Your meeting notes, written before you close your laptop." |
| "Save up to 10 hours per week!" | "Less time managing. More time thinking." |

---

### Accent Color: Confirmed

**`#7C6FFF` (Violet)**

This is the right call. Violet occupies a space none of the major competitors own — Notion uses blue, Reclaim uses teal/green, Motion uses blue/dark, Superhuman uses red, Granola uses orange-adjacent warmth. Violet is calm, premium, and slightly unexpected without being disruptive. It reads as thoughtful and focused, which matches the brand.

No change recommended from Ingrid's placeholder.

---

### Logo Treatment

**Wordmark only (initial phase)**

The name "Neutral" reads cleanly at all sizes. A wordmark-only approach is correct at this stage — it avoids over-designing something that hasn't shipped yet and keeps the nav uncluttered.

**Wordmark spec:**
- Font: Inter, weight 600
- Tracking: `-0.03em` (tight, premium)
- Color: `--text-primary` in light mode / `--text-primary` in dark mode
- Size: 18px in nav
- No icon, no mark, no symbol (for now)

**Future consideration:** A simple geometric mark (a small square or circle, half-filled) could be developed for favicon/app icon use once the product is shipped. Do not design this now.
