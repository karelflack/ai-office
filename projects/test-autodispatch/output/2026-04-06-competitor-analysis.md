# Competitor Analysis — Note-Taking App
**Date:** 2026-04-06 | **Author:** else

---

## Competitor Overview

| App | Positioning | Target Audience | Key Features | Pricing |
|-----|-------------|-----------------|--------------|---------|
| **Notion** | All-in-one workspace | Teams & individuals, startup to mid-market | Databases, wikis, kanban, docs, AI assistant | Free (personal); Plus $10/mo; Business $15/user/mo; Enterprise custom |
| **Obsidian** | Local-first knowledge graph | Power users, researchers, developers | Markdown, bi-directional links, graph view, plugins, offline-first | Free (personal); Catalyst $25 one-time; Commercial $50/user/yr; Sync $4/mo add-on |
| **Bear** | Focused writing, beautiful UI | Apple ecosystem users, writers | Markdown, tags, multi-level nesting, iCloud sync, clean design | Free (limited); Pro $2.99/mo or $29.99/yr |
| **Evernote** | Capture everything, everywhere | Professionals, cross-platform users | Web clipper, OCR, templates, tasks, calendar integration | Free (very limited); Personal $14.99/mo; Professional $17.99/mo |
| **Apple Notes** | Built-in simplicity | Casual Apple users | iCloud sync, collaboration, checklists, Quick Note, locked notes | Free (included with Apple ecosystem) |
| **Roam Research** | Networked thought, bi-directional linking | Academics, researchers, writers | Daily notes, block references, graph database, queries | $15/mo or $165/yr (no free tier) |

---

## Detailed Positioning

**Notion** dominates the "team workspace" category. Its flexibility is its strength and its weakness — it can do everything, which means onboarding is slow. Recent AI features ($10/mo add-on) are shallow. Retains users through collaboration lock-in.

**Obsidian** owns the power-user/privacy niche. Local storage resonates strongly with developers and researchers wary of cloud lock-in. The plugin ecosystem is vast. Steep learning curve; limited out-of-the-box for non-technical users.

**Bear** has the best writing experience in the iOS/macOS space. Clean, opinionated, and fast. No web app, no Windows — deliberately Apple-only. Loyal but geographically constrained audience.

**Evernote** is in decline. Once the category leader, it has lost significant market share to Notion and Obsidian. The rebrand and price increases (2023) damaged trust. Still used by long-term subscribers via inertia, not enthusiasm.

**Apple Notes** is the "good enough" option for casual users. Free and frictionless if you're already in the Apple ecosystem. Not a product people evangelize — they use it because it's already there.

**Roam Research** is a cult product. High price, no free tier, aggressive positioning. Pioneered bi-directional linking. Has lost ground to Obsidian (free, local) and Logseq (open-source). Declining mindshare.

## Gaps and Opportunities

| Gap | Observation |
|-----|-------------|
| **Onboarding vs. power** | Notion is powerful but complex; Bear is simple but limited. A product that offers progressive disclosure — simple by default, powerful when needed — has no clear owner. |
| **Collaboration + privacy** | Obsidian users want local-first but often need to share with teammates. No product does both well. |
| **Affordable team tier** | Bear is personal-only. Obsidian commercial licensing is confusing. Notion's team tier is expensive for small teams. A $5-8/user/mo team product with a clean UX is under-served. |
| **AI that isn't gimmicky** | Notion AI is expensive and shallow. Users want AI that understands their notes, not a chatbot bolted on. Deep, contextual AI is an unsolved problem. |
| **Cross-platform with quality UI** | Bear (Apple-only) has the best UI. Cross-platform tools (Notion, Obsidian, Evernote) all feel inconsistent across platforms. A genuinely high-quality cross-platform experience is rare. |

---

## Summary Recommendation for Nora (Pricing)

The market has two dominant price anchors: **free** (Apple Notes, Obsidian personal) and **$10-15/user/mo** (Notion, Evernote). The middle segment ($3-8/mo) is occupied only by Bear (Apple-only) and is largely uncontested for cross-platform products. The most defensible position for a new entrant is a **freemium model with a ~$6-8/mo individual tier and ~$8-10/user/mo team tier**, undercutting Notion while signaling quality above Apple Notes.
